# 마담제이 동작 — Flow 이미지-투-비디오 프롬프트
# 방식: Flow에서 "이미지 → 비디오"로, 아래 입력 이미지 + 프롬프트를 넣으면
#       Veo가 캐릭터를 그대로 유지하며 자연스럽게(관절·좌우 다 맞춰) 움직입니다.
# 스타일 고정 문구가 모든 프롬프트에 들어가 있어 라인아트가 안 깨집니다.
# 참고: [[feedback-autoveo-best-method]] 단일세션+클립보드 붙여넣기

# ── 공통 스타일 앵커(프롬프트에 이미 포함됨) ──
# "minimal 2D flat line-art animation, keep the exact character design
#  (brown bun hair, coral vest reading 선생님, white skirt, white shoes),
#  soft cream background, clean hand-drawn look, character identical every frame,
#  smooth natural motion, no realism, no style change"

=====================================================================
[1] 인사 (waving hello)   입력: assets/characters/madam_jay_base_front.png
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The same minimal 2D flat line-art teacher character stands centered, keeping her
exact design (brown bun hair, coral vest reading 선생님, white skirt, white shoes).
She smiles warmly and raises her right hand to wave hello twice in a friendly
greeting, then lowers it. Clean hand-drawn animation, smooth natural arm motion,
soft cream background, character identical every frame, no realism, no style change.

=====================================================================
[2] 걷기 (walking)   입력: assets/characters/madam_jay_base_front.png
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The same minimal 2D flat line-art teacher character walks with a gentle, natural
stride, legs alternating correctly and arms swinging softly in opposition, a calm
confident teacher walk. She keeps her exact design. Clean hand-drawn animation,
smooth natural gait, soft cream background, character identical every frame,
no realism, no style change.

=====================================================================
[3] 가리키기 (pointing at the board)   입력: assets/characters/madam_jay_pointing.png
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The same minimal 2D flat line-art teacher character raises her left arm and points
toward a lesson board on the side, elbow bending naturally, holding the point while
looking that way, then gestures once. She keeps her exact design. Clean hand-drawn
animation, natural arm and hand motion, soft cream background, character identical
every frame, no realism, no style change.

=====================================================================
[4] 설명 (talking / explaining)   입력: assets/characters/madam_jay_presenting.png
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The same minimal 2D flat line-art teacher character talks and explains, making
small natural hand gestures with both hands, head nodding slightly, a warm friendly
teacher explaining a lesson. She keeps her exact design. Clean hand-drawn animation,
subtle lively motion, soft cream background, character identical every frame,
no realism, no style change.

=====================================================================
[5] 앉기 (sitting down)   입력: assets/characters/madam_jay_base_front.png
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The same minimal 2D flat line-art teacher character sits down onto a simple chair
with a smooth natural motion, keeping her back straight and hands resting on her
lap. She keeps her exact design. Clean hand-drawn animation, natural sitting motion,
soft cream background, character identical every frame, no realism, no style change.
