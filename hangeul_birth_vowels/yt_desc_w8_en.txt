Learn Korean numbers, dates, and time. Understand the difference between native numbers (하나·둘·셋) and Sino-Korean numbers (일·이·삼), counting things and age, big numbers, prices and phone numbers, telling time (hour=native, minute=Sino), months, days, and days of the week — all step by step.
An easy Korean conversation lesson for beginners. Just knowing numbers makes daily life much easier.
Set in Gwangjang Market, Seoul. Learn together with your teacher!
