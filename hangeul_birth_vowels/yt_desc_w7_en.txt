Learn Korean greetings and self-introduction. From everyday greetings like 안녕하세요 (hello), 감사합니다 (thank you), 천만에요 (you're welcome), 죄송합니다 (sorry), 반갑습니다 (nice to meet you), 오랜만이에요 (long time no see) and 잘 지냈어요? (how have you been?), to introducing your name, country, job, age, home, and hobby with '저는 ○○입니다'.
An easy Korean conversation lesson for beginners — watch daily and it sticks.
Set in Insadong, Seoul. Learn together with your teacher!
