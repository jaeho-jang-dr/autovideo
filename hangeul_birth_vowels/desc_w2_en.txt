Korean consonants are shaped after the parts of the body that make each sound — the mouth, tongue, and throat (the principle of iconic shapes). From the five basic consonants, to adding strokes for stronger sounds, to combining a consonant and a vowel into one block — today you'll learn the 10 basic consonants and your first Korean words.
