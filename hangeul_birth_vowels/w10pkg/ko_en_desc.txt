Learn Korean '쇼핑·가격 표현' easily and fun by Gwangalli's Diamond Bridge! Master 얼마예요 (how much?), 이거 주세요 (this one, please), 결제 (payment), and 할인 (discount) — plus 이거·그거·저거 (this / that / that over there), paying by 카드 (card) or 현금 (cash), and 좀 깎아 주세요 (please lower it) — the phrases you actually use in a real shop, with InJoon.

🌏 Korean & English narration + subtitles in 5 languages (한국어 · English · 日本語 · 中文 · Español) supported — please select from the audio/subtitle tracks.

📖 What you'll learn in this lesson
• Key phrases: 얼마예요 (ask price) · 이거 주세요 (choose) · 결제 (pay) · 할인 (discount)
• Real lines: 이거 얼마예요? · 오천 원이에요 (it's 5,000 won) · 지금 할인 중이에요 (on sale now)
• Extras: 이거·그거·저거 주세요 / 카드·현금으로 결제할게요 / 좀 깎아 주세요

💡 How to study: ① Listen → ② Repeat aloud → ③ Create sentences with examples → ④ Use it in a real shop
