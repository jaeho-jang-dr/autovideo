A full review of 24 weeks of Korean, gathered in one place at Dongdaemun Design Plaza in Seoul. All seven classmates appear together.

We start in the classroom and find out what '수료' (completion) means, then walk through the underground ruins hall, the exhibition space, the design path, the plaza, the grass hill and the rose garden, bringing back the expressions we learned along the way — letters and numbers, words for the street, then words for feelings and thoughts. We end back in the classroom with a small ceremony: '축하합니다' (congratulations) and '고맙습니다' (thank you), and everyone says '앞으로 계속' (keep going) together.

Beginners and intermediate learners are both welcome. Subtitles include romanization of the actual pronunciation (with liaison and tensing reflected) for those studying in Hangul only.
