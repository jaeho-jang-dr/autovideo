Learn to stay calm and handle sudden emergencies in Korean — walk through Seoul's '이태원' (Itaewon), where cultures from around the world meet, with Injun and pick up the 'real Korean' for when you feel sick, get hurt, or lose something.
You'll learn to describe how you feel with '아프다' (to be sick) and '다치다' (to get hurt), find a '약국' (pharmacy) and a '병원' (hospital), call '119' and '112', and ask for help with today's core patterns '___을 잃어버렸어요' (I lost ___) and '도와주세요' (please help me). Plus how to clearly report 'what happened' and 'where' on an emergency call, and short roleplays at a pharmacy and a lost-and-found.
You'll also feel that calm matters most in a crisis, and the warm Korean culture where helping one another is taken for granted. Beginners and intermediate learners welcome!
