🚨 A few phrases that save the day in an emergency!
• '아파요' / '다쳤어요' — say how you feel
• '___을 잃어버렸어요' — I lost ___
• '도와주세요!' — please help me
• '112' (police) · '119' (fire/ambulance) — free, 24 hours
