🍁 Hello! We climbed Seoraksan, ablaze with red autumn leaves, with Jieun to learn how to say our thoughts logically in Korean.
Master the three beats — open with '제 생각에는' (in my opinion) → give a reason with '왜냐하면' (because) → close with '따라서' (therefore) — and your claim, reason, and persuasion all fall into place. Plus agreeing and disagreeing politely with '동의하다' and '반대하다'.
