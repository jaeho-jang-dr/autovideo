Learn to voice your thoughts logically in Korean — climb Seoraksan, ablaze with red autumn leaves, with Jieun and pick up the 'real Korean' for stating opinions clearly and persuading with warmth.
You'll learn a four-beat rhythm: open with '제 생각에는' (in my opinion), state a claim with '저는 ~다고 생각해요' (I think ~), give a reason with '왜냐하면 ~' (because ~), and close with '따라서 ~' (therefore ~). Plus '동의하다' (agree) and '반대하다' (disagree) done politely, the '-다고' indirect quotation, and a short debate on cable car vs hiking.
You'll also feel the secret of persuasion — 'empathy first, reason second' — and the Korean '예의' (courtesy) kept when sharing opinions. Beginners and intermediate learners welcome!
