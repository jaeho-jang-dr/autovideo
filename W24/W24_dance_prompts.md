## 공통 꼬리표 (네 클립 모두 붙인다)

```
STYLE: Flat cartoon illustration, bold clean black outlines, flat colours, evening light turning
to neon night. @stickman @zollagirl @zollaman stay hand-drawn ink line figures; @jieun @injun
@madamjay @teacherjay stay fully coloured cartoon people. Do not blend the two styles.

★GLOW: @stickman's ink lines glow like a bright neon tube - a soft luminous halo around every
line, clearly visible in the dark. Only @stickman glows; nobody else does.

ANATOMY LOCK: each character has exactly one head, two arms with two hands (five fingers each)
and two legs. No extra or missing limbs, no fused bodies, no floating hands. Every character stays
whole and fully inside the frame.

HEIGHT ORDER (keep consistent): @injun tallest, then @zollaman, then @teacherjay and @stickman,
then @jieun, then @zollagirl, then @madamjay shortest.

NEGATIVE: no text, no letters, no numbers, no signage lettering, no logos, no watermark.
16:9, 1280x720.
```

## 클립 1 — 점화 (0~8초 · 약 20박)

> 첫 프레임: 새로 생성

```
Wide evening shot of a large open plaza in front of a silver curved modern building. A ring of
ordinary onlookers surrounds an empty circle in the middle.

Beat 1-4: @teacherjay stands alone at the centre and throws out the first move — right arm raised
high, waving twice on the beat.

Beat 5-12: the move spreads outward like a wave, half a beat apart, left to right.
@zollaman copies it, then @zollagirl, then @stickman, then @injun, then @jieun, then @madamjay.
Each one picks it up a fraction later than the one before, so the wave visibly travels.

Beat 13-20: all seven now move on the same beat in a loose semicircle facing the camera, while the
onlookers begin clapping and step closer, tightening the ring.

★@stickman's glowing lines are noticeably brighter than everything else in the frame.
```

## 클립 2 — 짝 댄스 (8~16초 · 약 20박)

> 첫 프레임: **클립1의 마지막 프레임**

```
The same plaza, sky turning orange. The seven dance inside the ring of clapping onlookers.
Each one dances DIFFERENTLY - this is the point of the shot.

★PAIR 1 — @zollaman and @zollagirl HOLD BOTH HANDS and dance together as a couple: they swing
their joined arms side to side on the beat, spin once around each other, then lean back pulling
against each other's hands, laughing. Their ink lines swing loosely with the motion.

★PAIR 2 — @injun and @jieun FACE EACH OTHER and mirror each other's steps: @injun throws a sharp
arm angle, @jieun answers with the same move smoothly on the next beat, back and forth like a
call and response. They never touch, they just mirror.

@madamjay turns to the onlookers, claps overhead and pulls them into the rhythm; the nearest
people copy her.

@teacherjay conducts from the middle, pointing to each pair in turn on the beat.

★@stickman leaves the group — his glowing body walks straight INTO the crowd and starts moving
between the onlookers, weaving through them, arms out, leading them. Where he passes, the people
he touches start dancing too.

Camera holds wide so all seven stay in frame.
```

## 클립 3 — 한글 만들기 (16~24초 · 약 20박)

> 첫 프레임: **클립2의 마지막 프레임**

```
The camera lifts up and back into a HIGH OVERHEAD SHOT looking down at the whole plaza at dusk,
neon lights switching on around the square.

★@stickman, glowing bright in the dark crowd, runs through the mass of people drawing a path with
his own light — he is leading them into a formation. The onlookers he passes turn and follow him,
then stop and stand where he places them, so a shape starts to build out of the crowd.

Seen from above, the standing people slowly form two large Korean letter shapes on the plaza
floor: the block 한 on the left and the block 글 on the right. @stickman's glowing trail traces
each stroke in the correct writing order - top stroke first, then down, then the bottom - and the
line of people fills in behind him.

The other six (@teacherjay @zollaman @zollagirl @injun @jieun @madamjay) dance in the open space
between the two letter blocks, still moving on the beat.

The letters are formed by BODIES AND THE GLOWING TRAIL ONLY - do not draw or overlay any printed
text, font or graphic letters.
```

## 클립 4 — 완성과 정지 (24~32초 · 약 20박)

> 첫 프레임: **클립3의 마지막 프레임**

```
Still looking down from above, the crowd formation is complete and holds for one beat — the two
letter shapes clearly readable, @stickman's glowing figure standing at the very last stroke,
arms raised.

Then the camera drops back down to eye level at the front of the plaza, now fully night with
coloured neon reflecting off the ground.

The seven run forward out of the formation and line up in one row facing the camera, in height
order from left to right: @injun, @zollaman, @teacherjay, @stickman, @jieun, @zollagirl,
@madamjay.

On the final beat they finish together — both arms thrown wide and up in a big V — and hold
perfectly still, smiling straight at the camera. The crowd behind them freezes mid-cheer with
arms up. @stickman still glows, the brightest thing in the frame.

The camera pulls back and rises, the neon-lit plaza opening out around the frozen row of seven.
```
